# Employee Unit Payments v6

- Each employee now has independent payout percentages.
- Default unpaid-unit split remains 65% employee / 30% equipment payoff / 5% company.
- Default paid-unit split remains 95% employee / 5% company.
- Admin percentages must total 100% for each stage.
- Equipment payoff is calculated unit-by-unit. Money may partially fund the next unit, but a unit only changes to the paid-off payout rate after its full unit cost is covered.
- Example: 50 chairs at $14 each and $50 recognized chair rental revenue at a 30% payoff rate creates $15 payoff: 1 chair fully paid and $1 progress toward chair #2. On the next eligible rental, 1 chair earns at the paid-off rate and the remaining chairs still use the unpaid-unit split.
