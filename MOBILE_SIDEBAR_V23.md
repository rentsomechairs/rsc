# Mobile Sidebar v23

A hamburger menu is now available on phone/tablet widths (up to 1024px).

- Tap ☰ in the top bar to slide the full navigation in from the left.
- Tap ×, the dark page overlay, press Escape, or choose a page to close it.
- The drawer uses `100dvh`, iOS momentum scrolling, and safe fixed positioning.
- The full sidebar navigation is available to both admins and employees.
- Employee bottom navigation from v22 remains available as a quick shortcut, while the full sidebar drawer provides every allowed employee navigation item and utilities.
- Desktop navigation is unchanged above 1024px.

No Firestore or Firebase Function changes are required.
